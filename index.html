The rules that make this work

  • • Effort on drills: 3–5/10 max. Trying too hard is the #1 mistake — soft, long exhales (“jelly-belly”), never neck.
  • • Strength: RPE ≤ 8 — always 1–2 perfect reps left in the tank. Add ≤5 lb/side/week on shoulder work.
  • • Order matters: daily drills → isometrics → plyos → strength (Lower Limb p18). Drills first always — they’re the prescribed warm-up, and within the drills the release/rolling sequences run first, in their exact listed order ("do it in this specific order each time", APT p35; Recal p11–12).
  • • Splitting = two complete passes, never half a sequence: morning carries the bigger share (APT p21–45: "2 sets in the morning and 1–2 at night"), evening is a lighter full pass that doubles as your warm-up. Drill volume tops out around 5 sets/day (Recal p10).
  • • Honesty note: the cardio schedule (Zone 2 + 4×4) is our design — none of the four eBooks address cardio.
  • • Phases advance every 2 weeks. Miss days? Get ~14 real days of a phase before moving on. Don’t skip weeks.
  • • Daily-drill sets can be split (some AM, some PM). On strength days, 2 sets of drills = your warm-up and count toward the daily total.
  • • Time budget: weeks 1–6 run ≈ 7.5–9.5 h/week all-in (daily drills are the bulk of it); weeks 7–8 drop to ≈ 4.5–5.5 h. Every day shows a ⏱ card with exact minutes — one-session vs. AM/PM split — and a latest start time so you always finish by 7:15 pm, two hours before your 9:15 bedtime.
  • • De-duplication: when two books prescribe the same exercise (same video) in the same weeks, it appears once with a 2-IN-1 badge — do it once.
